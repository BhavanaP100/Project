Elderly Care Management

Real-Time Health Monitoring

Monitor vital signs such as heart rate, blood pressure, and oxygen levels remotely. Ensure elders are safe and in optimal health without the need for constant physical visits.

Automated Appointment Scheduling

Easily schedule medical checkups, therapy sessions, and other important appointments with reminders sent automatically to caregivers and elders.

Activity Planning & Engagement

Create and manage personalized activity plans to promote physical fitness and social interaction among elders, improving their overall well-being.

Comprehensive Digital Health Records

Keep track of medical histories, prescriptions, test results, and other important documents in a secure digital format for easy access by caregivers and medical staff.
